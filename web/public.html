Data & permissions overview

Dashboard login uses Discord identity and guild permissions. Configuration, ticket metadata, moderation and progression records are stored per server. Private ticket transcripts may be saved for support operations. Optional AI sends explicit owner requests to OpenAI. Voice XP uses Discord voice-state activity rather than audio recording. This overview is not a finalized privacy policy.

For questions or issues, contact the server owner or bot operator. Never share account credentials or bot tokens.

# TOV4R Bot — Terms of use (draft, operator review required)

V5.0 release candidate. These are proposed community service terms, not a finalized legal contract.

1. Use TOV4R only in servers where you have the relevant authorization and in accordance with Discord's terms and community guidelines.
2. Do not use TOV4R for harassment, spam, unauthorized data access, evasion of permissions, or disruption of communities.
3. Server managers are responsible for reviewing setup previews and selecting appropriate channel, moderation, and role settings. Do not assign staff permissions without authorization.
4. AI is optional and disabled by default per server. After owner opt-in, members may ask questions using /ai; only the owner may use /ai_admin to administer the server. Requests may be sent to OpenAI. Do not submit secrets or personal information. AI results can be wrong and must be reviewed.
5. Availability and feature behavior depend on Discord, hosting, configuration, and optional external providers. No uptime guarantee is made in this draft.
6. Report security issues or misuse to the server manager or bot operator. A verified public contact, support route, suspension process and applicable legal terms must be established by the operator before public release.

The operator must review these terms, identify the responsible party and contact details, and verify compatibility with applicable law and Discord's developer requirements before publication.
